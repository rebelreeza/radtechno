Category 01

Campaign Intelligence

Turn every launch into institutional knowledge. Make the next campaign smarter than the last.

⬡ Competitive Moat

UC-01

Cross-Store Campaign Pattern Matching

After a campaign closes, AI extracts its success fingerprint - audience trust index, scarcity responsiveness, price sensitivity, sell-through velocity, content format - and encodes it as structured attributes. It then scans all 40 stores and ranks them by similarity, identifying which stores share enough characteristics to replicate the playbook and why.

Business outcome

"Creator A's October drop cleared in 18 hours. Stores 7, 12, and 31 share 74–87% of the same audience attributes. Here's an adapted campaign brief for each, tuned to their specific product mix and posting cadence."

BigQuery Claude API Social data Compounds over time

How it works - step by step

  • 01Campaign closes. AI agent queries BigQuery for all orders, UTMs, sell-through rate, AOV, customer profile during window.
  • 02AI synthesizes success factors into a structured fingerprint - not just metrics but the WHY behind them.
  • 03Fingerprint is encoded into `store_profile` table in BigQuery as comparable attributes.
  • 04Similarity query runs across all 40 store profiles. Ranked match list generated.
  • 05For each matched store, AI generates an adapted campaign brief using that store's own product and audience data.
  • 06Output delivered to ops team as a structured recommendation with confidence score and reasoning.

UC-06

Audience Saturation Detector

AI monitors repeat purchase rate, time-between-orders, and new-vs-returning customer ratio per store over time. When a store's audience starts showing saturation signals - declining new customer rate, same customers buying every drop - it flags that the creator may need a new audience growth strategy before the next campaign.

Business outcome

Catch audience fatigue before revenue decline. Prompt the creator to expand reach before the well runs dry.

UC-07

Price Sensitivity Profiling

AI models the price elasticity curve for each store's audience - the point at which conversion rate drops meaningfully as price increases. Different creator audiences have completely different price ceilings. Knowing the ceiling per store optimizes product pricing at launch and prevents leaving margin on the table.

Business outcome

"Store 7's audience converts well up to $89. Above that, conversion drops 40%. Store 12's ceiling is $140. Price accordingly."

Elasticity modelingPer-store

Business outcome

Audience-specific and timing-optimized.

UC-18

Margin Leakage Detector

AI continuously monitors gross margin per store, per SKU, per campaign - comparing realized margin against expected margin and flagging unexplained gaps. Root cause analysis runs automatically: is the leak in COGS, fulfillment cost, discount depth, return rate, or shipping overages? Surfaces it before it compounds.

Business outcome

Most margin leakage at this scale is invisible without deliberate monitoring. AI makes it audible before it becomes a quarterly surprise.

Creates specific bundle recommendations per store with projected AOV lift based on comparable bundle performance elsewhere in the portfolio.

Category 07

Risk & Anomaly Detection

Know when something is wrong before it becomes a problem. Protect the business at 40-store scale.

UC-25

Inventory Sync Integrity Monitor

AI continuously compares inventory counts between Shopify and Odoo across all 40 stores and flags discrepancies above a defined threshold in real time. Classifies the likely cause - sync failure, manual adjustment, return processing lag - and routes to the right fix. Catches oversell risk before a customer experiences it.

Business outcome

At 40 stores, one sync failure per store per month is 40 potential oversell incidents. AI makes each one visible immediately instead of after the complaint.

Real-time monitorOps integrity

UC-26

Revenue Anomaly Explainer

When a store's revenue deviates significantly from its expected baseline - up or down - AI automatically generates an explanation rooted in actual data: was it a campaign, a social post that went viral, a competitor event, a Shopify outage, a product going out of stock? Answers the "why" before the CEO asks it.

Business outcome

Revenue spikes and dips are currently explained by whoever noticed them. AI makes every anomaly self-explaining and documented.

Root cause AICEO visibility

UC-27

Fraud & Abuse Pattern Detector

AI models normal order behavior per store and flags anomalous patterns - discount code abuse, unusual order clustering from the same IP ranges, return fraud patterns, or reseller behavior (bulk orders of limited-availability items). Surfaces patterns that are invisible in single-store views but obvious when looking across 40 stores simultaneously.

Business outcome

Reseller and fraud patterns at one store often appear at others shortly after. Catching the pattern once protects all 40.

Anomaly detectionCross-store
